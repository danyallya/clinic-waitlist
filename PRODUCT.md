# Product brief

Problem: Show approximate waits and offer canceled slots to the next eligible patient.

Audience: small clinics and patients

## First release

One user role, one core record (appointments) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Keep health data out of the queue; confirm clinic flow and patient consent before notifications.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
